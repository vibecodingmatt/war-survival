TAKE A BREATHER

Holding position.

Shoot left for +1 soldiers, center for enemies, and right for stronger guns. Finish floating weapon goals before they pass. A full squad hides recruits until you lose soldiers.
WASD or arrows move your squad. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 choose a lane. Space calls artillery.Drag on the bridge to move. Lift your finger to stop. You can tap Artillery with a second finger while moving.
Openings vary by sector. Linked rifts offer reinforcements or seven powers, including giant ducks and wind-up tanks. Some pit two powers against each other. Shoot one; its partner closes. Full squads get a shield option. Shoot pods for temporary ammo: fans, corkscrews, ricochets, sonic shells, or a gold Rainbow Rush jackpot. Dodge red boss swings; shields protect against being thrown.
